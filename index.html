Marriage License — Action Required After the Wedding 📋

As officiant, Kai is legally responsible for signing and returning the marriage license. Venus and Matthew will obtain their license from Beverly Hills Courthouse before the wedding.

1
Sign the license at the ceremony
After Venus and Matthew exchange vows, Kai signs the marriage license as officiant. The two witnesses also sign — confirm with Venus and Matthew in advance who the two witnesses will be. All signatures must be completed on the day of the wedding. Do not leave the venue without signing.
2
Return within 10 days
California law requires the signed license to be returned to the issuing county clerk within 10 days of the ceremony. The wedding is Nov 10 — so the hard deadline is November 20, 2026. Do not wait on this.
3
Option A — Return in person (recommended)
Drop off the signed license at the Beverly Hills Courthouse, 9355 Burton Way, Beverly Hills, CA 90210. Hours: Mon–Fri 8:30 am – 4:30 pm. No appointment needed for drop-off. This is the fastest way to confirm receipt and get immediate proof that it has been filed.
4
Option B — Mail it
Mail the signed license to:
Los Angeles County Registrar-Recorder/County Clerk
Marriage Licenses, P.O. Box 1208, Norwalk, CA 90651-1208

If mailing, use a trackable service (USPS Certified Mail or Priority Mail) and mail immediately after the wedding — no later than Nov 12 — to ensure it arrives within the 10-day window. Keep the tracking receipt as proof of timely submission.

⚠️ Important: Do not alter, white-out, or use correction fluid anywhere on the license — any errors must be corrected by the county clerk and cannot be fixed by the officiant. Keep the license flat, dry, and in a safe place until it is returned. The signed license is what legally makes Venus and Matthew married, so guard it carefully on the night!
